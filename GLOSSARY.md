# Room check: Glossary

Checks a piece of furniture or decor against a person's room and their taste, and says whether it works there.

## Taste

**Confirmed rule**:
A taste rule the person has agreed to, written as what (a tone, color or material) / works when / doesn't work when. Binding within its scope.
_Avoid_: preference, constraint

**Proposed rule**:
A taste rule the agent suggested that the person hasn't confirmed. Ignored in judgment.
_Avoid_: draft rule, suggested rule

**Like**:
A style word or reference the person gave (for example "paper lanterns", "calm, natural materials"). Soft: can raise a score, never lower one.
_Avoid_: rule, preference

**Speaks to**:
A confirmed rule speaks to a piece when its what covers the piece's tone, color or material, or its conditions address the same concern as a built-in principle.

## Judgment

**Built-in principle**:
General design practice the judge applies to every room regardless of style (context, legibility, scale and proportion, cohesion). Fills in where confirmed rules are silent.
_Avoid_: default rule

**Legibility**:
Whether a piece reads as itself against its surround (floor, rug, wall, the pieces beside it) through tone, texture, sheen, form or layering.
Also called the built-in contrast principle.
_Avoid_: contrast rule, floor contrast

**Verified fact**:
A value read from the product page (price, dimensions, material, availability).
_Avoid_: guess, estimate

**Fact flag**:
A warning that follows from verified facts alone (over budget, price not found, sold out), never from taste.
_Avoid_: code flag

**Block**:
A verdict that rules a piece out for this room, either because it breaks a confirmed rule or because it merges into its surround.
_Avoid_: reject

**Watch**:
The main concern on a card, paired with a Try (a concrete fix). A piece with only a Watch is workable, not blocked.
_Avoid_: warning, concern

**Over-block**:
A block on a piece that no confirmed rule rules out and that doesn't truly merge with its surround.
_Avoid_: false positive
